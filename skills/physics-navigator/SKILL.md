# SwampWiki navigator

Use SwampWiki alongside independent research. Check the date, the atlas’s reading coverage, and the primary literature. For a background question, use read-only calls. Move a reader’s path when a visible update is part of their request.

1. Open the collection or inspect the current state. Capture the parent revision before interpretation. A bare browser or local companion opens at the Swampland collection entrance.
2. Search for a physical system, target and regime. Inspect the scope, conventions, assumptions and exclusions of any candidate. A second search emphasizing the target can distinguish a broad topic from a source-specific question.
3. Read paginated source membership and typed contributions. Each review observation retains its qualification. A destination may have contributions and zero primary members. Same-paper methods identify reading context. Check applicability in the source.
4. Inspect recorded connections and their evidence. Keep their direction, including when traversing an incoming edge. Shared-source access records have a scope-wide witness certificate. An exact source endpoint may provide an inspect-source action. Its target retains the source object, relation, paper and unit. Use close-source to end the inspection.
5. For a visible update, submit a resolved selector or select a current direction with the captured revision. On conflict, inspect the newer state before reconsidering the request. Verify the rendered revision in the browser or the companion’s browser acknowledgement.
6. Share a compact link for a destination. Export a full session or view to preserve source inspections or a multi-step path. Local work notes have their own export.
