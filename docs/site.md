# About SwampWiki

SwampWiki is a text navigator for the Swampland and related quantum-gravity literature. Open the collection, choose a question, and follow a recorded connection. Each destination includes its scope, assumptions, sources and reading limits. Search matches titles and aliases. The Sources section links to individual papers.

The frozen source collection contains 6,478 bibliographic records, including 6,477 with extracted descriptions. Its 8,473 question descriptions and 17,937 method descriptions date to the November 5, 2026 snapshot. The November 17 organization accounts for 8,436 questions with reviewed placements and 37 with unresolved placements. Adjacent fields appear where they belong to the curated collection. Use independent literature searches for subsequent papers and other perspectives.

A connection carries its recorded type and direction. Scope-wide shared-source links and collection entries provide reading routes. Open the review record to inspect a claim’s basis. Same-paper method context requires a separate judgment about applicability.

Back and the path buttons return to earlier steps. New path opens the collection entrance. Each destination has a shareable address. Work notes stay in your browser and can be exported separately. Download a full view or session to retain an exact source-method inspection.

## Origin

The first SwampWiki distribution derives from the November 17 Eigenquestions atlas at commit 387e95edb11aca883cbf1d05ceba9513bfd1e364. The build removes the general physics vocabulary, its subject portal, and its graphical layouts. Stable scientific destination IDs, source records, typed links and their evidence come from the frozen release. The version manifest links the derived catalog to its upstream hashes.
